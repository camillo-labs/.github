### Camillo Labs

Will Leikauskas and Cameron Mills build personal AI systems: agents that capture what you do, remember it, work overnight and hand the results back.

CAMILLO holds Cam, (W)ill and Mills. A Roman *camillus* was a priest's young assistant, and Giulio Camillo built a memory theatre in the 1530s. An assistant with a memory: that is the work.
